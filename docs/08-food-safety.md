# Food Safety Boundary

ReServe is a coordination and matching system, not a food-safety certification service.

## Donor responsibilities

The donor should provide accurate information about food identity, allergens, preparation/packaging, storage, deadline, and other applicable handling details.

## Platform responsibilities

ReServe may validate required fields, preserve timestamps, and match operational capabilities. It should not present a match score as proof that food is safe.

## Recipient responsibilities

The recipient decides whether to accept the donation and applies its own inspection, handling, storage, and regulatory procedures.

## Production requirement

Before launch, review applicable federal, state, and local food-donation and food-safety requirements with qualified counsel and relevant food-safety authorities.
